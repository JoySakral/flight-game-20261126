SKY COURIER

Valley Run · a pocket flight game

You are the courier of the Green Valley. Take off from Meadowfield strip, thread the glowing rings, mind your fuel, and bring her home on the far airstrip.

W / S throttle  ·  ↑ / ↓ pitch  ·  ← / → roll  ·  mouse: steer  ·  G gear  ·  B brakes  ·  P pause  ·  R restart

Click a mission, or press 1 / 2 / 3, to fly.

An original game · art, sound and code made for this entry · no network needed
